# JourneyProof pivot

JourneyProof explored WebMCP-native spatial journey rehearsal for AEC
practitioners using bounded Rhino geometry analysis. It remains intact in the
separate local repository `senibina-webmcp-journeyproof` and is not migrated,
renamed, or rewritten by Apartment Intelligence.

The challenge direction changed because Apartment Intelligence offers a clearer consumer
job: help a Singapore resident understand sunlight conditions at a particular
existing HDB apartment. Its audience, input data, geometry pipeline, evidence,
analysis, and deliverables differ enough to require a separate repository and
current product owner.

The following principles carry forward:

- use domain-specific deterministic geometry rather than generic prompt-to-3D;
- let agents coordinate structured operations without granting design or
  evidentiary authority;
- require humans to confirm spatial facts that public data cannot establish;
- make WebMCP and manual UI actions share visible state; and
- distinguish proposals, implemented behavior, computed evidence, and accepted
  outcomes.

JourneyProof remains historical context, not a current owner of Apartment Intelligence.
