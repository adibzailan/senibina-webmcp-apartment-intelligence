## Generation prompt (Senibina illustration contract, from the Living Atelier studies)

Base prompt, shared by all ten:

> Sparse British editorial pen-and-ink illustration with loose, slightly wobbly black contours,
> economical detail and architectural awareness. Pure white background, monochrome black line,
> generous white space, fine hatching only where a shadow explains the form. Elevated
> three-quarter view of one Singapore HDB development, drawn from the attached photograph so
> that its real massing, silhouette, wing arrangement, storey rhythm and sky-garden or void-deck
> openings are recognisable. At most two small figures at ground level for scale, one tree or
> planter, no cars. No colour, wash, text, arrows, labels, logos, signage, watermark,
> photorealism, polished vector treatment, manga styling, CAD rendering, drop shadows or clutter.
> Horizontal 4:3 composition, 1200 x 900, the building filling about two thirds of the frame with
> a level horizon and sky left empty.

Source photos are reference only and are not committed; only the generated illustrations are.
